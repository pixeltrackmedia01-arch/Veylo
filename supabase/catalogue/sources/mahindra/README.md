Sources: auto.mahindra.com and mahindraelectricsuv.com (every variant with its ex-showroom
price and the colour list with paint codes), and the official brochures (spec lines printed as
text, kept under "Brochure specs").

**Price city:** Mahindra shows one ex-showroom price for India (no city selector), so the
"Surat" figure is that national ex-showroom price. Surat on-road prices still need RTO and
insurance added.

**Gaps:** Mahindra's website has no spec sheet or feature list, and its brochures show features
as tick icons (not readable as text). So per-variant features, mileage and most specs are not
included; XUV 7XO and XUV400 brochures have no text specs at all. Bolero Neo Plus has no
brochure on its page. Fill these from CarDekho/CarWale if needed. XUV 3XO REVX variants are
listed under XUV 3XO. XUV400 has a single variant left on sale.
