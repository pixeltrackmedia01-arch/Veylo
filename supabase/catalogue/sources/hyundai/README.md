# Hyundai catalogue (official data)

Collected on 2026-10-30. Prices are **ex-showroom** in ₹ lakh — Surat, Gujarat.

Sources:
- **hyundai.com/in** — every variant with its Surat ex-showroom price (`standard` and
  `dual_tone` paint), and the engine specification sheets (power, torque, gearbox,
  dimensions, brakes, suspension, tyres, fuel tank).
- **Official brochures** (hyundai.com, Sept–Oct 2026 editions) — per-trim feature lists
  (Safety, Exterior, Interior, Comfort, Infotainment …), exterior colours, and the specs for
  Creta Electric and Ioniq 5 (hyundai.com has no spec sheet for them). Features marked
  "(optional)" are optional on that trim.

**Not published by Hyundai:** claimed mileage (km/l) for petrol/diesel/CNG models and boot
space for most models — fill these from ARAI figures or CarDekho/CarWale later. EV range
is included for Creta Electric.

Tucson and Kona Electric are no longer in Hyundai India's price list, so they are treated
as discontinued. The Ioniq 5 price list still calls its only variant "72.6 kWh", while the
current brochure lists an 84 kWh battery — confirm with a dealer.
