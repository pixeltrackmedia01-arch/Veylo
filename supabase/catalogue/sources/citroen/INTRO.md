Source: Citroën India's price service (the data behind citroen.in's Check Price tool) — every
variant with its ex-showroom price (one national price list; the "Surat" figure is that price)
and the official brochure link per model.

**Gaps:** variant names don't always state fuel/gearbox (treated as petrol manual unless named);
no machine-readable spec or feature data; colours are not included.
